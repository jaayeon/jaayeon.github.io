About Me

I am a 4th-year PhD student at KAIST AI in the BISPL Lab, advised by Prof. Jong Chul Ye.

My research focuses on diffusion reinforcement learning for diffusion models. I am currently working on autoregressive video diffusion, with a focus on long video generation and controllability.

Feel free to reach out if you would like to connect or discuss research!
